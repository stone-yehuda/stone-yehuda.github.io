01 / WHAT I BUILD

Agents wired into the revenue stack

I build agents that plug into the tools revenue teams already use. They pull from the CRM and the warehouse, do one job, hand off to a person when it needs judgment, and write the results back. 02 / HOW I WORK

How I build

A few rules I hold my own work to.

01

Measure the business outcome

I judge an agent by pipeline, time saved and data quality. One good-looking output doesn't count.

02

Keep a person in the loop

Agents draft and prepare, and people approve anything customer-facing. An agent only gets more autonomy once the results show it's ready.

03

Trace everything

Every run leaves a trace, failures turn into test cases, and every change runs against evals before it goes live.
