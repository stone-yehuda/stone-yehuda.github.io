02 / HOW I WORK

How I build

01

Tie it to a number

Every process I build answers to a business metric: pipeline, time saved or data quality.

02

Prompting and context first

Proper prompting and context are the key to agentic success. Each agent gets clear instructions and the data and examples it needs for its job, and nothing extra.

03

Report on every step

I own the reporting on every process I build, down to each step of an agent's flow, so there's clear visibility into accuracy, efficiency and gaps.
